14. FAQ

What is Tulum Innovation Fest?

A four-day gathering combining innovation, entrepreneurship, capital, culture, wellness and destination experiences in Tulum.

Who is the festival for?

Founders, investors, executives, creators, technology leaders, ecosystem builders and people interested in emerging innovation.

Is it only focused on crypto or Web3?

No. Tulum Innovation Fest evolved from Tulum Crypto Fest into a broader innovation platform covering AI, capital, future cities, digital assets, entrepreneurship, culture, climate and human connection.

What is Whale Tank?

Whale Tank is the official curated founder experience and startup showcase of Tulum Innovation Fest.

Does applying to Whale Tank guarantee participation?

No. Applications are reviewed and selected according to business quality, traction, scalability and fit with the program.

Are accommodation and travel included?

Accommodation is included only in selected packages. Flights, local transportation and additional hospitality experiences should be specified separately.

Where will the festival take place?

The program will take place across selected venues in Tulum, including Ikal Arena and the Whale Tank founder venue.

How can my company become a partner?

Submit the partnership form with your objectives, target audience and approximate investment range. The festival team will propose the most relevant format.
