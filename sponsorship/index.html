FAQ

Commercial questions, answered directly.

Can packages be customized for an enterprise objective?

Yes. The published tiers establish the level of ownership. Final inventory, content, hospitality, data capture and reporting are scoped around the partner objective.

Is category exclusivity available?

It can be included in selected strategic packages when the category remains available and the commercial scope supports it.

How will partnership performance be measured?

The measurement plan is approved before activation and can cover registrations, attendance, meetings, opt-in leads, demos, engagement, content performance and reporting.

Does the festival guarantee leads or sales?

The team can design data capture and curated introductions, but commercial outcomes depend on the offer, activation, audience fit and partner follow-up.

When should a partner confirm?

Strategic partners should confirm early enough to secure category availability, content planning and production. Lead times vary by package.

Is the festival focused only on crypto?

The festival evolved from Tulum Crypto Fest into a broader platform covering AI, capital, future cities, digital assets, entrepreneurship, culture, climate and human connection.
