What is Tulum Innovation Fest?

A four-day gathering combining innovation, entrepreneurship, capital, culture, wellness and destination experiences in Tulum.

Who is the festival for?

Founders, investors, executives, creators, technology leaders, ecosystem builders and people interested in emerging innovation.

Is it only focused on crypto or Web3?

No. The festival covers AI, capital, future cities, digital assets, entrepreneurship, culture, climate and human connection.

Are accommodation and travel included?

Accommodation is included only in selected packages. Flights, local transportation and additional hospitality experiences are separate unless explicitly stated.

How can my company participate?

Attend as a team, apply to Whale Tank, or contact the festival team to explore partnership and activation formats.
