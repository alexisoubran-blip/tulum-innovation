01

Terms & conditions

Whale Tank Program · participation terms

01Selection & participation

This offer is for startups selected for Whale Tank. Selection for the program does not guarantee a place in the Final Showcase. Advancement follows the program evaluation process; payment does not buy an investment outcome.

02Program value & scholarship

The principal participant package is valued at USD 5,800. Main Sponsor / Program Partners cover a USD 3,300 acceleration scholarship, leaving USD 2,500 payable by the startup. The scholarship covers program services; it is not an investment in the startup.

03Accommodation & transfers

The package includes private accommodation, breakfast, round-trip airport transfers and transfers between venues. The booking confirmation must specify the hotel, check-in/out dates, included nights and transfer schedule. Festival dates alone do not define the accommodation period.

04Additional team members & VIP

Each additional member of the same startup pays USD 1,000, including when sharing the room of the principal participant. The complimentary VIP upgrade replaces the standard festival access; the USD 1,500 figure is the VIP pass value.

05Investment & success fee

USD 1.5M–3M in letters of intent is not guaranteed funding or an allocation per startup. Investment depends on evaluation, due diligence and investor approval. Whale Tank receives 5% of each investment actually received and secured through its efforts, under the participation agreement.

06Access & program updates

Ticket details must match the ID of the attendee. Optional or capacity-limited experiences require the appropriate access and availability. Session times and program arrangements may change; the organizer will communicate material updates and applicable options. The included benefits remain as described in this offer.

07Cancellations, refunds & transfers

For purchases through Ticket Fairy, the event-specific purchase conditions and platform policies apply, subject to applicable law. Refunds depend on organizer terms; platform service fees are generally non-refundable unless law requires otherwise. Ticket resale is available only if enabled. Direct Whale Tank payments require their own written cancellation terms.

08Scope & participant support

Whale Tank participation, hospitality and the investment success fee are program-specific conditions. Ticket Fairy processes ticket purchases; it does not provide the acceleration program or guarantee capital. Program and booking questions: innovation@likegroup.io.
