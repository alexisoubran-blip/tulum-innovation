Terms & conditions

Whale Tank Program · participation terms

01Selection & participation

This offer applies to selected startups. Program admission does not guarantee advancement to the Final Showcase or an investment. Presentations and progression follow the program evaluation process.

02Participation fees

With stay: USD 2,500 for the principal participant and USD 1,000 for each additional member. Without stay: USD 1,500 for the principal participant and USD 720 for each additional member of the same startup. Fees are per person.

03Scholarships & organization support

Both packages include fully funded acceleration and VIP / Executive access for every registered team member, including additional members. These benefits are sponsored by the organization and Program Partners. Support amounts remain confidential; the participation fee is not the full value of the experience.

04Accommodation & transport

The with-stay package includes accommodation, breakfast and airport/venue transfers. Additional members share the principal participant's room. Booking details must specify the hotel, nights, check-in/out dates and transfer schedule. The without-stay package does not include accommodation or hotel breakfast.

05Investment & success fee

Letters of intent are not guaranteed funding or an allocation per startup. Investment requires investor approval and due diligence. Whale Tank receives a 5% success fee on each investment actually received and secured through its efforts, as set out in the participation agreement.

06Festival access & program updates

Attendee names must match identification. Optional or capacity-limited experiences depend on access rights and availability. Schedules and arrangements may change; material updates and applicable options will be communicated. Included benefits remain as described in this offer.

07Cancellations, refunds & transfers

Purchases through Ticket Fairy follow event-specific conditions and platform policies, subject to applicable law. Refunds depend on organizer terms; platform fees are generally non-refundable unless law requires otherwise. Resale applies only if enabled. Direct program payments require written cancellation terms.

08Program support

Whale Tank participation, hospitality and the success fee are program-specific conditions. Ticket Fairy processes ticket purchases; it does not provide acceleration or guarantee funding. Contact: innovation@likegroup.io.
