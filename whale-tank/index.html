A founder journey, not a pitch contest

From startup signal to investor-ready company.

Whale Tank combines mentoring, business preparation, pitch training, networking and investor matchmaking inside Tulum Innovation Fest 2026.

01
Diagnose

Business + market readiness

Problem validation, customer discovery, business model, market clarity and go-to-market strategy.

02
Prepare

Investment readiness

Fundraising strategy, growth logic and the structure needed to present the company coherently to capital.

03
Pressure-test

Pitch, feedback, pitch again

Mock pitches, pitch preparation, Q&A drills and repeated feedback from people who understand the room.

04
Connect

Investor matchmaking

Curated introductions, roundtables and one-on-one conversations with investors and strategic ecosystem partners.

05
Present

Final stage at Tulum Innovation Fest

Five finalists present in front of VC funds, angel investors, family offices, CVCs, innovation leaders, media and the festival community.
